📖 The origin story

One day, a certain four-coloured-squares cloud suite went down. Millions of office workers suddenly had nothing to do. Most went for coffee. Dave went for an existential crisis:

"Wait… what if Downdetector goes down?"

The suite came back in two hours. Dave is still building this page. Nobody has told him.

🏛️ Hall of fame: times the watchmen actually fell

  1. 2 Jul 2019
    The Great Regex Incident. One bad firewall rule at Cloudflare spiked CPU to 100% worldwide for about 30 minutes. Downdetector showed a 502, so nobody could check why Downdetector showed a 502. Lesson: never deploy a regex on a Tuesday. Or ever. source
  2. 6–7 Jun 2020
    The 53-Minute Wobble. A backend issue gave Downdetector a partial outage. People couldn't report outages because the outage-reporting site had an outage. Peak 2020 energy. source
  3. 18 Nov 2025
    The Big One. A Cloudflare outage took down X, ChatGPT, Spotify… and Downdetector. Thousands of people tried to report that everything was down, to a site that was down. Someone then built a Downdetector downdetector. Then someone built a Downdetector downdetector downdetector. We are part of the problem. source
  4. 5 Dec 2025
    The Sequel Nobody Asked For. Just 17 days later, fresh Cloudflare issues took Downdetector down again. Like a horror movie, but the monster is a config file. source
  5. Next time
    ??? Nobody knows when. But we'll be here. Watching. Blinking occasionally.
